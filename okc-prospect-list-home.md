## The scoring model

Unchanged from the first list, so the two are directly comparable.

| Input | What it measures | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| **Category** | Dwell time × captivity. | 25–100 |
| **Convenience premium** | Straight-line feet to the nearest food or convenience alternative. >2,640 ft = +45. <400 ft = +0. | 0–45 |
| **Route cost** | Distance from the anchor. Under 1 mi = +20, over 3 mi = +4. | 4–20 |

**Total = category + convenience premium + route cost.** Max 165. Walk-minutes assume 280 ft/min.

## Skip these

- **The four country clubs and golf courses** (The Greens, Gaillardia, Quail Creek, Lake Hefner) — every one has clubhouse food service. Cut before ranking.
- **Apartment complexes** (The Shores, Springs at Memorial) — residents, not a captive workforce. Cut before ranking.
- **Convenience stores** (a 7-Eleven came through the pull mis-tagged as a warehouse) — they are the alternative you're competing with.
- **Self storage** (3 sites, all Expressway Self Storage) — the model liked them for being isolated, but one or two people work there. Scored down 60 points, kept on the list only for completeness.
- **AAC Advanced Aircraft Coatings** — scores 121, but the business runs 2 to 5 people. A machine can't pay for itself on five customers.
- **The 16 K-12 schools** in the ring — wellness rules, district approval, dead summers. Same call as the first list.
- **The Lighthouse** at 3333 W Hefner is stale in the map data: that building is now **TB Fitness Club**, (405) 849-9173, 40,000 SF with courts, golf simulators and a recovery area. Call it under the right name.
